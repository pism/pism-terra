# A subsection

Headings in a section file are subsections: `#` here becomes `\subsection`, `##` a `\subsubsection`.

Equations are LaTeX, numbered and labelled as usual:

$$
\frac{\partial H}{\partial t} = \dot{a} - \nabla \cdot \mathbf{q}
\label{eq:mass}
$$

and referred to with `\ref`, as in Eq. (\ref{eq:mass}).
